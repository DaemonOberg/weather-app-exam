About Weather App

Weather App provides current weather conditions and three-day forecasts for selected cities in Sweden.

How It Works

Select a city on the home page to view its current temperature, wind speed, and a three-day forecast with maximum temperatures, minimum temperatures, and precipitation.

Weather Data

Weather information is provided by Open-Meteo . Weather data is made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.
